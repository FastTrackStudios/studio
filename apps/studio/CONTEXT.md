# Studio

The app a band, church or school uses to plan its songs and sets, chart them, run them live and mix them, on every screen.

## Language

### People

**Account**:
A person's sign-in, made by anyone who downloads Studio or opens it on the web.
_Avoid_: User, login

**Organization**:
A band, church, school or company that owns sessions and setlists and has a team; any account can create one, and joins someone else's only by invitation.
_Avoid_: Org (in prose), workspace, team (the team is the org's people)

### Songs and sets

**Session**:
One song's live document: its form, arrangement, tracks, mix and chart, kept as one `.session` file and synced between everyone who has it open.
_Avoid_: Project, song document, song file

**Setlist**:
The plan for a gathering: a group of sessions in running order, with each one's key and leader.
_Avoid_: Set (in prose), show, playlist

**Opening a session**:
Bringing one session up in Studio, alone or as the current item of an open setlist.

**Generated chart**:
The chart Keyflow makes from a session's own markers: its sections and measure numbers, with no chords or notes until someone writes them.

### Playing together

**Room**:
An organization's lasting place to play together, reached by one link that never changes; whatever its leader loads (a setlist or a lone session) everyone in it follows, without reconnecting.
_Avoid_: Session (that is one song), live session, gathering

**Leader**:
The one person in a room who loads what is open and drives the transport; everyone else follows and may still edit the open session.

**Guest**:
Someone in a room without an account in its organization: follows and plays but does not edit, and is recognised as the same guest each time they come back.
_Avoid_: Anonymous user, visitor

**Joining**:
Opening a room's link; when the room is live you follow its leader, and when it is not you open its setlist on your own to practise.
