# Cooked scenes

Ignition's looks and effects as myDMX Steps scenes, recorded off the engine at 10 fps (`ignition-viz` example `cook_dmx`) and written by `mobile_rig`. A cycle is where the output repeats after a 2 s warm-up; *fitted* means the period falls between frames and the closest whole-frame repeat was used. A *one-shot* (an effect that ends held: a hit, a fly-in) is cooked from its first frame to where it lands, held a second, and loops on the desk like the rest. RockStrip colour comes from the wall bars (`recolour_strips`). Step times are 1/25 s ticks.
