A setlist's share link on a phone

Five screens at 390 by 844 and one at 1280 by 800, for docs/design/share-link-setlist.md. The titles, artists, keys and lengths are the recital's. The covers, the lyric lines, the chart's bars and the song's sections are stand-ins: the vault is private, and these files are in a public repository.

Each frame is a page of its own: open one at phone width to see it as it would sit on a phone. They follow the system's light or dark theme, as the app does.
