Omnisphere's Bandpass Juicy 12db filter (995 factory filters) resonates: its resonance knob had been fitted as a fixed Q, so a resonant band-pass layer played up to ~12 dB quiet. Measured every 0.1 against the plugin, Q rises 0.72 → 11.8 and the peak gain is 20·log10(Q) (the raw SVF band output); the filter gains a measured resonance → Q table and a raw band-pass output, and now follows the plugin within ~0.4 dB of peak gain from resonance 0 to 1 — a factory drone that played 6 dB quiet is within 0.6 dB (signal)
