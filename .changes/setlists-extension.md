The setlists Architect extension (`studio-setlists`): setlists and song libraries in the navigator's two-stage sidebar (setlists, or libraries and their categories, on the left; their songs on the right; each song a scope tab in the "Song" screenset), `STUDIO_SETLISTS_*` actions to make, edit and share setlists through the app's own vault, Bases and share links; compiled into an Architect build or run as a process the Architect app installs (session)
