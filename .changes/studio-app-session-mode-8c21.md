Studio has two modes: the launcher (Home, Setlists, the set pages and the sidebar) and the session, a set open for playing that takes the whole window. Its top bar is the setlist bar (the set's progress, the song before and after, a tab per song with its own progress), the arrangement is its main view, and its foot holds back-to-sets, the transport and the views (overview, chart, lyrics, editor, arrangement, mixer, FX, setup). Natively the whole set is loaded into studio engine and a song's tab goes to it there; the mixer drives the engine's faders, mute and solo, in the session or in a window of its own. `STUDIO_SESSION_OPEN`, `STUDIO_SESSION_CLOSE`, `STUDIO_SESSION_VIEW`, `STUDIO_MIXER_WINDOW` (session)
