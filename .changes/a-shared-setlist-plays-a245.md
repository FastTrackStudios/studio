A shared setlist plays like a playlist: one big Play, each song a row (title, artist, key · tempo · meter, its recording's length) that plays in place, and each song's chart as the now-playing view (with Architect's playlist player; on v0.12.2 a row plays from the page as before). A song's note keeps its recording's length (session)
