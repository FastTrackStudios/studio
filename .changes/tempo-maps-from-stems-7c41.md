Tempo maps from a song's stems, in Studio: the `tempo-map` library (beats from the drum stems, snapped to hits, the backbeat's phase, bar-1 candidates, one tempo point per bar, meters other than 4/4 and meter changes, the repair of added and dropped beats, a map measured and two compared, a benchmark against accepted maps) and the `studio.tempo-map` extension that runs it on any song of a set; the engine's `TEMPO_MAP_SET` (a whole tempo map with time signatures and bar 1) and `TRACK_MARKERS_SET` (many track markers in one step) (session)
