Omnisphere's other Timbre mode (`tbmd` 0) plays above 0.65 as the plugin's does at its root: a steep high-pass whose corner rises with the knob (~105 Hz at 0.66 to ~1.8 kHz at 1, measured on a piano and a cello) — its error on the harmonics below 1.2 kHz roughly halves (at 1: 43 → 8–10 dB); 0.35–0.65 stays flat as measured, and the low end (below 0.35), where the two sources part ways, is not modelled (signal)
