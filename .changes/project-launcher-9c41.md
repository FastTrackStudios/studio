The project launcher (`studio.launcher`), linked into Studio: an Open section in Architect's navigator (recent projects, setlists, rigs) and a setlist as Architect's media list in a pane or a window of its own. A press opens the set or a song in Studio's engine, lays the panes out (Stage, Rehearse or a saved layout, remembered per setlist) and points the chart and the lighting at the song, which also follow when the engine goes to another. At launch it shows when the engine has nothing open; its window closes when something opens. A setlist's note keeps a note per song and the rigs it is played with. Needs Architect's media list with a press and a tile (architect feat/media-list-press). Its design is docs/design/project-launcher.md (session)
