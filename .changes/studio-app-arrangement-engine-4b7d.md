The arrangement view is back in Studio: a song's sections, tracks (folders and order) and clips, read from its one `.session`, with a time ruler, a seekable progress bar and a playhead that follows playback; reached from a setlist file's song rows, the top bar and `STUDIO_ARRANGEMENT`. Natively a set plays through `studio engine` (attached, or spawned from the daw extension with its own folder and port), its transport driving Now Playing; without one the timer moves the playhead and the page says why (`STUDIO_ENGINE`) (session)
