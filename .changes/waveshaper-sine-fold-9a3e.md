The waveshaper gains a sine wave-folder (`fold`, with a `pre_gain` into it), and Omnisphere's Waveshaper type 0 — the factory's most-used — plays as one, measured on a sine: its depth folds (`w = 122·depth`), its Gain drives the folder rather than the output, and a sampled layer folds by its own level; type-0 levels and spectra that ran 3–12 dB off across depth and gain now sit within ~1–4 to depth 0.75 (signal)
