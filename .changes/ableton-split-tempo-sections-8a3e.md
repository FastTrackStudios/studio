A Live set split into songs plays at its own tempos and has its sections: a tempo step is one point at the tempo it takes (the click, plugins and MIDI took the first of two points at one moment, and the transport's map would not build), a song cut at a tempo change starts at its own tempo, and each song's section cards (the MIDI clips on a `SECTION…` track) become its sections in their colours (a card named after the song is its Intro), with COUNT-IN, SONGSTART and SONGEND marks (session)
