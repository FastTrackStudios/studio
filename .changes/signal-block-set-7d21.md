A Signal block's parameters are set by their ids with `SIGNAL_BLOCK_SET` (`{track, block, params, bypass?}`): any parameter, an EQ's bands past the six its slots show and a comp's own makeup among them, in its own units, landing at once whether the song plays or not — in the chain read, in a render started next, in one undo step. FX_SET on a Signal instance is in the next chain read too, instead of one up to a quarter second old (session)
