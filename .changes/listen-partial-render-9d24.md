Listening for an agent. LISTEN renders offline only what feeds one point of a track (its input, before its FX, into or out of any FX, before or after its fader) and answers its loudness, true peak, crest, third-octave spectrum, stereo correlation and each dynamics FX's gain reduction, with a waveform and a spectrogram PNG; a listen whose points nothing upstream has changed since is answered from what an earlier one heard. LISTEN_HITS averages a drum piece's hits from its hit markers — the sum's attack, decay, head and tail spectra and spread, and per mic its offset and polarity against the reference mic, its correlation with the other mics and what it adds to or takes from the sum — with a PNG of every mic's averaged hit (session)
