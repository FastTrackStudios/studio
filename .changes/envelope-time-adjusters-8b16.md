Omnisphere's envelope time adjusters play (`AENVPARAMS` / `FENVPARAMS` `a`, `d`, `r`, set in ~5 % of the factory patches): each multiplies its segments' durations by `1 − value` — attack to the peak, decay to the sustain point, release after it — measured on a 2 s attack and a 0.8 s release to within 0.03 s (signal)
