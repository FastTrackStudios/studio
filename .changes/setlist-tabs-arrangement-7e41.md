A setlist's share link has its own tabs at the bottom of the web app: Setlist, Charts (the chart of the song playing), Lyrics and Arrangement, with the round button playing and pausing. Arrangement is a new read-only view: the song playing on a timeline with its sections and sung lines and the playhead through them; a tap plays from there (session)
