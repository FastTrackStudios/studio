A setlist's share page reads as a streaming app's playlist: its page is one studio-setlist fence with each set's name a line of it (## Set), drawn as one list with section labels, each song a row with its number, an art tile (initials on a colour from its title, the song's art over it when the link has some), title over artist · key · tempo · meter, length and a chart icon; no play buttons; a song with no recording stays in the list and opens its page. A song note's art is its art: property (Song::art), kept on import (session)
