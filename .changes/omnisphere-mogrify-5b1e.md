Omnisphere's Mogrify plays: a sample layer's fresh notes start part-way into their sample (measured on the MT-240 Piano at its root key, 25 ms at 0.05 up to 257 ms at 1) and rise in over 2 ms, from the patch's own value and from `mogrify` routes (about 31 % of factory patches set it); against the plugin the attack gap drops from 7–9 dB to 3–5 dB. A sample soundsource takes `tune` routes too, so a pitch route lands on a sampled layer as it does on a synth one (signal)
