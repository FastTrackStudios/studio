Studio engines signed in to one account find and admit each other through Architect's devices (the account pass against the issuer, iroh by n0 and on the LAN), with no address typed. Each says what it runs (tracks, which rigs); any one follows another's song (switching to the rig's set that has it), section, rig cues and transport, and an engine nobody drives (an iPad) follows the one playing tracks by itself. The rig books sync between the engines directly, with no Task and no hub. `studio peers` lists them; `follow`, `unfollow`, `sets`, `set`, `song`, `transport` and `cues <set.als> --write` (a Live show's cue track as each song's rig cues) drive it (signal)
